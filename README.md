# Aerosol–Cloud–Climate Interactions: Bangladesh and the Northern Bay of Bengal

This project investigates feasible satellite-based research on aerosol–cloud interactions using GIS, remote sensing, and statistical analysis.

**Status: evidence review and proposed study design. No observational data have been downloaded, no analyses have been executed, and no empirical results are available.** The study location, final research question, and analysis specification remain subject to the researcher's selection and a data-coverage pilot.

## Research before manuscript preparation

1. Review the regional literature and define a measurable question.
2. Audit data coverage and retrieval quality.
3. Freeze the main analysis and validation plan.
4. Acquire and harmonize a small pilot dataset.
5. Analyze, visualize, and check results, including null and contradictory findings.
6. Expand only if the pilot supports a defensible study.
7. Consider manuscript preparation after the results have been reviewed.

The provisional starting recommendation is a meteorology-adjusted analysis of shallow, liquid-cloud microphysics over the northern Bay of Bengal and Bangladesh's coastal outflow. This is a recommendation, not a final author decision.

## Scientific boundaries

- Aerosol optical depth (AOD) is a column optical measure, not a direct measurement of cloud condensation nuclei (CCN) or surface PM2.5.
- Satellite-derived droplet number concentration is an estimate with restrictive assumptions.
- Aerosol–cloud correlations, even adjusted for weather, do not by themselves establish causation.
- Cloud-top temperature, radar reflectivity, and precipitation are not direct measurements of updraft speed.
- Regional cloud radiative effect anomalies are not automatically anthropogenic effective radiative forcing.
- Null findings and failed sensitivity checks will be retained.

## Reproducibility

Keep acquisition manifests, exact product versions, QA rules, code, compact derived tables, figures, and run records in this repository. Large raw satellite archives should be stored in an appropriate external data repository, with persistent links and checksums here. Credentials must never be committed.
