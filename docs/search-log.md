## Screening decisions and limitations

Use journal publications with a located title, year, journal and DOI; distinguish perspectives/reviews/opinions from new observational or modeling results. Prioritize direct regional precedents and methods that alter interpretation or feasibility.

Some Nature/Wiley full pages returned access/cookie failures. Publisher-indexed abstracts and institutional author records were used where available. Do not describe these papers as fully read. Read scopes are recorded per paper in [literature.md](literature.md).

SciSpace returned an anomalous duplicate author record for the Sharma paper. It was rejected in favor of the publisher/institutional author list. This illustrates why discovery-service metadata alone is insufficient.

Bibliographic citation-graph context was inspected through available article references and related-paper searches, but no systematic forward/backward citation graph was harvested. Search results were neither exhaustively exported nor screened with PRISMA; there is no completeness claim.
