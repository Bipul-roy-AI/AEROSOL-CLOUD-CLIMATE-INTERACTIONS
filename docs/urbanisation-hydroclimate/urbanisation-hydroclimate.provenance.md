# Urbanisation hydroclimate: provenance and verification

- Search conducted: 2026-11-04 UTC (system clock); cutoff is that date, not the end of November. Selected narrative review; no formal protocol-driven systematic screening or count of all records retrieved. Plan: `outputs/.plans/urbanisation-hydroclimate.md`.
- Search routes: web searches on global urban rainfall, heat, groundwater, runoff and recent literature; OpenAlex, Semantic Scholar, Crossref, PubMed/PMC; publisher pages and citations. The reviewers consulted the primary and review sources listed in the three research notes below, plus direct searches noted there. AlphaXiv CLI required login and was blocked. OpenAlex anonymous API lacked a key. USGS page gave 403 and a NERC archive DNS resolution failed; the numerical claim from the latter was not used. A local Python `requests` import failed; Crossref was checked with the listed scholarly database tool instead.
- Accepted: the 15 references in the review, selected by relevance, source availability and methodological contrast. Exact Crossref records were retrieved for all 14 research papers, and the EPA page was accessed; see the complete reference audit. DOI-resolver requests returned HTTP 406 in the verifier's environment, so direct resolution was blocked rather than confirmed, while relevant publisher pages and metadata were checked where accessible. Six core papers had an additional source-content audit. Exact link identity is not equivalent to reproduction. Quantitative checks drew on primary results where feasible; Austin is attributed to a secondary review of a water-budget estimate.
- Considered but not accepted as quantitative evidence: 2026 China urban precipitation paper and 2026 drought papers surfaced in search but their full details were not independently checked; NERC search snippet for Anderson et al. 2022 (DOI 10.1029/2021WR030742) was not directly inspected, so no quoted flow percentage. Mazrooei et al. 2021 ET paper surfaced but accessible primary details could not be checked in this run. These are omissions, not adverse judgments of the papers.
- Independent numerical verifier: `outputs/.drafts/urbanisation-hydroclimate-verifier.md`. Its summary characterises Hao's 215 mm/year differently from the primary text; direct inspection of the paper's results states 'mean annual ET difference is about 215 mm' for the 20-site comparison (https://www.nature.com/articles/s41612-023-00479-z); the review uses the primary-text interpretation while flagging sample selection. Global/European Guccione results were separately checked to prevent a geographic error.
- Research files consulted: `outputs/.drafts/urbanisation-hydroclimate-research-parallel.task-1.md`, `.task-2.md`, `.task-3.md`. Copies with descriptive names are in `/workspace/output/urbanisation-hydroclimate/`.
- Limits: abstract, metadata, selected primary results and model descriptions were inspected where accessible; calculations, raw data, full supplementary material, all regional papers and uncertainty intervals were not independently reproduced. English-language/online and large-city biases remain. Search is current only through the search date.
- Reviewer and full reference audit: companion `urbanisation-hydroclimate-review.md` and `urbanisation-hydroclimate-reference-audit.md` in the requested directory. The reviewer found no FATAL issues and four MAJOR issues: the draft was revised to explain shared IMERG dependence and product disagreement, fixed station classification, flood proxy limitations, and unsupported geographic/ET generalisations. Remaining open questions include city-list overlap, exact flood-proxy construction, and a region-balanced station inventory. All 15 citation numbers were checked against the reference list after revision.
