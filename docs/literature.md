# Literature and evidence notes

Targeted search: 2020–2026; prepared 2 November 2026. Nineteen identifiable journal publications are included. This is a starting corpus, not an exhaustive or formally systematic review. Full author lists are in [references.bib](../references.bib). The presence of a bibliographic record does not mean full methods or supplements were read.

## Forcing and synthesis

### Bellouin2020

Bellouin, N.; Quaas, J.; Gryspeerdt, E.; et al. (2020). **Bounding Global Aerosol Radiative Forcing of Climate Change.** *Reviews of Geophysics*, 58, e2019RG000660. [DOI](https://doi.org/10.1029/2019RG000660).

Research relevance: Foundational synthesis of forcing evidence; observational sensitivity and historical anthropogenic forcing are different quantities.

Read/access scope: publisher abstract/search excerpts and institutional citation. No independent re-analysis of the paper's data was performed.

### Im2026

Im, U.; Samset, B. H.; Nenes, A.; et al. (2026). **Aerosol-Cloud Interactions: Overcoming a Barrier to Projecting Near-Term Climate Evolution and Risk.** *AGU Advances*, 7, e2025AV001872. [DOI](https://doi.org/10.1029/2025AV001872).

Research relevance: Current perspective on the observation–modeling gap and emerging tools; not a new measurement of regional forcing.

Read/access scope: NASA GISS author/citation record and abstract. No independent re-analysis of the paper's data was performed.

### Stier2024

Stier, P.; van den Heever, S. C.; Christensen, M. W.; et al. (2024). **Multifaceted aerosol effects on precipitation.** *Nature Geoscience*, 17, 719–732. [DOI](https://doi.org/10.1038/s41561-024-01482-6).

Research relevance: Distinguishes well-founded microphysical effects from uncertain net precipitation responses across scales.

Read/access scope: publisher author list and abstract; subscription full text not read. No independent re-analysis of the paper's data was performed.

## Liquid clouds and sampling

### Glassmeier2021

Glassmeier, F.; Hoffmann, F.; Johnson, J. S.; Yamaguchi, T.; Carslaw, K. S.; Feingold, G. (2021). **Aerosol-cloud-climate cooling overestimated by ship-track data.** *Science*, 371, 485–489. [DOI](https://doi.org/10.1126/science.abd3980).

Research relevance: Model–satellite comparison cautions against extrapolating short-lived visible tracks to climatological cooling.

Read/access scope: author/institutional publication records and NOAA research summary; full paper not read. No independent re-analysis of the paper's data was performed.

### Manshausen2022

Manshausen, P.; Watson-Parris, D.; Christensen, M. W.; Jalkanen, J.-P.; Stier, P. (2022). **Invisible ship tracks show large cloud sensitivity to aerosol.** *Nature*, 610, 101–106. [DOI](https://doi.org/10.1038/s41586-022-05122-0).

Research relevance: Emissions and ship-position-based sampling finds cloud perturbations without visually identifiable tracks. Its regime/time sampling differs from Glassmeier et al.; do not assume one universal LWP response.

Read/access scope: publisher search excerpts and author/institutional records; full methods not read. No independent re-analysis of the paper's data was performed.

### Goren2025

Goren, T.; Choudhury, G.; Kretzschmar, J.; McCoy, I. (2025). **Co-variability drives the inverted-V sensitivity between liquid water path and droplet concentrations.** *Atmospheric Chemistry and Physics*, 25, 3413–3423. [DOI](https://doi.org/10.5194/acp-25-3413-2025).

Research relevance: An inverted-V LWP–Nd pattern alone is not evidence of causal aerosol adjustment.

Read/access scope: publisher abstract and introduction. No independent re-analysis of the paper's data was performed.

## Liquid clouds and vertical structure

### Li2026

Li, Z.; Painemal, D.; Feng, Y.; Zheng, X. (2026). **Advancing the quantification of aerosol-cloud interactions with the CALIPSO-CloudSat-Aqua/MODIS record.** *Atmospheric Chemistry and Physics*, 26, 7705–7720. [DOI](https://doi.org/10.5194/acp-26-7705-2026).

Research relevance: Vertically matched aerosol extinction improves the physical interpretation of aerosol–droplet associations relative to column AOD.

Read/access scope: publisher abstract and introduction. No independent re-analysis of the paper's data was performed.

Zhou, X.; Painemal, D.; Gettelman, A.; Feingold, G. ## Convection and updrafts

### Abbott2021

Abbott, T. H.; Cronin, T. W. (2021). **Aerosol invigoration of atmospheric convection through increases in humidity.** *Science*, 371, 83–85. [DOI](https://doi.org/10.1126/science.abc5181).

Research relevance: Idealized simulations identify an environmental-humidity pathway; this is not direct evidence of invigoration over Bangladesh.

Read/access scope: publisher/index abstract and MIT author record. No independent re-analysis of the paper's data was performed.

### Igel2021

Igel, A. L.; van den Heever, S. C. (2021). **Invigoration or Enervation of Convective Clouds by Aerosols?.** *Geophysical Research Letters*, 48, e2021GL093804. [DOI](https://doi.org/10.1029/2021GL093804).

Research relevance: Shows that freezing-related effects can weaken warm-based storms; challenges simple stronger-storm narratives.

Read/access scope: publisher abstract/excerpts and author records. No independent re-analysis of the paper's data was performed.

### Varble2023

Varble, A. C.; Igel, A. L.; Morrison, H.; Grabowski, W. W.; Lebo, Z. J. (2023). **Opinion: A critical evaluation of the evidence for aerosol invigoration of deep convection.** *Atmospheric Chemistry and Physics*, 23, 13791–13808. [DOI](https://doi.org/10.5194/acp-23-13791-2023).

Research relevance: Critical opinion/review identifying proxy, sampling, and meteorological-control problems. Read alongside studies reporting conditional effects.

Read/access scope: publisher abstract and selected text. No independent re-analysis of the paper's data was performed.

### Pimperl2026

Pimperl, H. E.; Igel, A. M. (2026). **Observational Evidence for Both Aerosol-Induced Invigoration and Enervation.** *Journal of Geophysical Research: Atmospheres*, 131, e2025JD046280. [DOI](https://doi.org/10.1029/2025JD046280).

Research relevance: Storm tracking over the Southern Great Plains finds associations conditional on aerosol size and environment. The authors explicitly do not claim observational proof of aerosol causation. The publisher lists A. M. Igel for this 2026 paper; earlier papers use A. L. Igel.

Read/access scope: publisher abstract, plain-language summary, author metadata and availability statement. No independent re-analysis of the paper's data was performed.

## Bangladesh and neighboring regions

### Ali2022

Ali, M. A.; Bilal, M.; Wang, Y.; et al. (2022). **Spatiotemporal changes in aerosols over Bangladesh using 18 years of MODIS and reanalysis data.** *Journal of Environmental Management*, 315, 115097. [DOI](https://doi.org/10.1016/j.jenvman.2022.115097).

Research relevance: Regional aerosol baseline and CAMS/MERRA-2 comparison. MODIS-reference agreement does not establish independent validation where reanalyses assimilate related observations.

Read/access scope: NASA MODIS and KNMI abstracts/citation records; full methods not read. No independent re-analysis of the paper's data was performed.

### Sharma2023

Sharma, P.; Ganguly, D.; Sharma, A. K.; Kant, S.; Mishra, S. (2023). **Assessing the Aerosols, Clouds and Their Relationship Over the Northern Bay of Bengal Using a Global Climate Model.** *Earth and Space Science*, 10, e2022EA002706. [DOI](https://doi.org/10.1029/2022EA002706).

Research relevance: CAM5–MODIS comparison shows sensitivity to meteorological constraints, especially humidity. Demonstrates that northern Bay aerosol–cloud analysis is an existing research area.

Read/access scope: publisher abstract/excerpts and author/institutional citation records. No independent re-analysis of the paper's data was performed.

### Kant2023

Kant, S.; Sarangi, C.; Wilcox, E. M. (2023). **Aerosol processes perturb cloud trends over Bay of Bengal: observational evidence.** *npj Climate and Atmospheric Science*, 6, 132. [DOI](https://doi.org/10.1038/s41612-023-00443-x).

Research relevance: Links elevated aerosol, atmospheric stability and low-cloud occurrence over northern Bay pre-monsoon conditions; independent attribution checks remain a useful research direction.

Read/access scope: publisher abstract, introduction and selected methods excerpts. No independent re-analysis of the paper's data was performed.

### Gulistan2024

Gulistan, N.; Alam, K.; Liu, Y. (2024). **Influence of covariance of aerosol and meteorology on co-located precipitating and non-precipitating clouds over the Indo-Gangetic Plain.** *Atmospheric Chemistry and Physics*, 24, 11333–11349. [DOI](https://doi.org/10.5194/acp-24-11333-2024).

Research relevance: Nearby regional example showing season and precipitation-regime dependence; observational sensitivities should not be equated with causal effects.

Read/access scope: publisher abstract and selected introduction. No independent re-analysis of the paper's data was performed.

### Jin2025

Jin, Q.; Huang, J.; Wei, J.; Pu, B. (2025). **Observational evidence of reduced Bay of Bengal lightning since 2020 linked to cloud responses to shipping emission regulations.** *npj Climate and Atmospheric Science*, 8, 350. [DOI](https://doi.org/10.1038/s41612-025-01256-w).

Research relevance: Directly relevant shipping–cloud–lightning precedent. WWLLN access and cross-year detection efficiency require checking before planning a replication; cloud changes are not direct updraft measurements.

Read/access scope: publisher-indexed abstract/search excerpts; full text and supplements not read. No independent re-analysis of the paper's data was performed.

## Interpretive tensions to retain

- **Glassmeier 2021 vs Manshausen 2022:** both concern ship-related cloud perturbations but use different sampling and timescales. Their contrasting implications for LWP/cooling do not justify a single universal response. Further full-method comparison is required; this is an advisor-inferred conditional difference.
- **Abbott 2021 vs Igel 2021 and Varble 2023:** humidity-mediated, longer-timescale responses differ from instantaneous/freezing-based invigoration claims. These mechanisms and definitions should not be pooled into a binary yes/no verdict.
- **Pimperl 2026:** evidence of conditional associations is not universal invigoration or proof of causation; the authors explicitly state this limitation in their plain-language summary.
- **Kant 2023 and Sharma 2023:** elevated-aerosol heating/stability and cloud microphysics are different pathways. A Bangladesh project must distinguish proposed mechanisms and identify what its observations can resolve.
- **Goren 2025:** an LWP–Nd shape consistent with a mechanism is not sufficient causal evidence. Shared retrieval inputs also need attention in any regional application.

These are bounded advisory interpretations, not a full pairwise contradiction audit. The researcher has not yet adjudicated them.

## Verification limits

Publication identity and DOI were located in publisher or author/institutional records. DOI resolution, correction/retraction checks and full-text reading were not programmatically audited as a batch. Avoid claiming that all papers are correction-free. Before the protocol is frozen, read the decisive papers and supplements, check publisher update notices and confirm all numerical or method-specific claims against the cited version. No unlocated or invented reference has been added as evidence.
